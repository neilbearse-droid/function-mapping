Forester is a board for mapping who owns what across a leadership team — and for planning how those responsibilities shift as the team grows. Use it as a current-state map and a forward-looking workforce plan.

Everything saves automatically in your browser on this device. To work with someone else, use Share, Comments & Suggestions.

1 · Getting started

The board shows one column per person or open role, and a card for each responsibility that person owns. To start from scratch you can edit the sample data, or hit Reset (top right) to clear everything on this device and build your own.

  • Add a column with Add teammate (Today) or Add role (future views).
  • Add a card with Add responsibility at the bottom of any column.
  • Drag cards between columns to change who owns what.

2 · The three time horizons

The tabs Today, In 6 months, and In 12 months are three independent plans. Editing one never changes another, so you can compare scenarios side by side.

Future views are most useful when they start from reality. Use Copy from Today (and the 12-month view can copy from 6 months) to seed a horizon with your current team, then make changes from there.

3 · People & open roles

A column is either a real teammate (it has a name) or an Open role — a planned vacancy, shown with a dashed outline. An open role can still own responsibilities, which is exactly how you justify a hire: pile the work onto the role you intend to fill.

  • Click a seat's name (or its pencil icon) to set a Role / title, a Name, and an optional target cost.
  • Leave the name blank to keep it an open role; add a name to turn it into a real teammate.
  • The Unassigned column collects responsibilities nobody currently owns.

4 · Responsibilities

Each card is one responsibility. Click a card to edit its function (the business area, e.g. Marketing), its description ("Owns…"), and its effort.

  • Reassign: drag a card to another column, or use its move (↩) icon — works on touch and keyboard too.
  • Effort (S / M / L): light / standard / heavy, worth 1 / 2 / 3 points. This feeds each person's load (below).

5 · Capacity, cost & risk

  • Capacity / load: each seat shows a load bar in effort points against a balanced load target you set, so overloaded people (and overloaded open roles) stand out.
  • Cost roll-up: give any seat an optional target cost and each view totals headcount cost and breaks out cost to hire (the sum of its open roles).
  • Key-person risk: anyone who alone holds two or more heavy (L) responsibilities is flagged — a single point of failure worth de-risking.

6 · The transition plan

Transition plan compares any two horizons and shows exactly what changes between them: roles to add, seats to fill, responsibilities that move, and the headcount-cost delta. It's the concrete path from your current team to the plan — useful for a board update or a hiring case.

7 · Views & generators

  • By person / By function: toggle the board between grouping by owner and grouping by business area. In the function view each card shows who owns it.
  • Responsibilities generator: pick a function and a granularity (Broad → Granular) to generate a starter list of responsibilities you can drag onto a person or drop into Unassigned.
  • Job-description drafter: open roles can generate a posting drafted from the responsibilities they own — editable in place, with a copy button.

The generator and JD drafter use a built-in library and templates — a starting point to edit, not a live AI model.

8 · Share, comments & suggestions

These let more than one person shape a plan. Set Your name once (in the Share dialog) — it attributes your comments and suggestions, and it travels with anything you share.

  • Share a plan: the Share button copies a link or downloads a .json file containing your whole board (all three horizons, costs, effort, comments and pending suggestions). Whoever opens it gets an editable copy. To open someone's plan, paste their link/code or import their file. This is a snapshot, not live sync — you draft, send, they tweak, send back.
  • Comments: every person and every responsibility has a 💬 thread. The icon shows a count; post with the button or ⌘/Ctrl + Enter.
  • Suggestion mode: toggle Suggest to propose changes instead of applying them. Moves, edits, adds and deletes become proposals (signed with your name) that show up as coloured banners on cards and dashed "ghost" cards for new responsibilities. Review them from the “N proposed” pill — Accept or Reject each, or accept/reject all. Great for weighing in without overwriting the owner's work.

10 · Data & privacy

  • Your edits are saved in this browser, per browser and per device. Two people opening the site each keep their own copy.
  • Nothing is sent to a server automatically. There are no accounts and no live shared source of truth yet.
  • Sharing is explicit: a link or file you generate is a snapshot of the plan at that moment — treat it like any document you'd email. Anyone with it can open the plan.
